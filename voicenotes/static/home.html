Dhwani · self-hosted · private · English

Speak a note. Keep it as a text file.

Dhwani (ध्वनि) means sound in Hindi and Sanskrit. It turns your spoken thoughts into text on your own machine, then saves each note as a .txt file in the folder you choose.

Record in your browser, check the words, and keep your notes where you can always find them.

Start a note How it works

Speak, don't type

Record up to 25 seconds at a time. The transcript lands in the note box, ready to review.

Plain .txt files

Each note is a text file in a folder you pick. Open it in any editor. Existing files are never overwritten.

Private by design

No account and no cloud. Speech recognition runs on your machine. Audio is never saved.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add a folder

    Paste the full path of a folder on your computer, for example /Users/you/Notes.

  2. 2

    Record

    Click Record, speak, then click Stop. The clip also stops by itself after 25 seconds.

  3. 3

    Review and save

    Fix any wrong word by hand, pick a folder, and save. Your note is a .txt file.

Your notes stay on your machine.

The page loads its styling from a CDN. Audio and note text never leave this computer.
